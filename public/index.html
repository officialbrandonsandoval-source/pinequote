What is real in this demo, and what is not

  • Real: the pricing engine is the live Iron and Pine price book. The segmentation math runs on the tile pixels in your browser. The client test yard reproduces the hand measured quote.
  • Real: leaf and needle work is blown to the tree line only. There is no haul away add on anywhere in this price book.
  • Demo stand in: the client tile is a sample tile drawn to the measured proportions of that yard, and the quote copy is written by local templates. The production wiring swaps segmentation to SAM 3.1 and copy to a Meta model through the Meta Model API. The code marks both swap points.
